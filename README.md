# Robotics TEAM KR

Robotics TEAM KR is a Korean robotics community comprising industry professionals, aspiring robotics engineers, and students who collaborate on open-source robotics projects.

## Overview

This repository serves as the GitHub profile and documentation hub for the Robotics TEAM KR community. Our members work together to advance robotics research, engineering practices, and open-source development across a broad range of robotics domains.

## Research and Development Areas

- Quadruped robotics
- Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMR)
- Manipulators
- Bipedal robotics
- Dual-arm robotic systems
- Robotic hands and end-effectors
- Humanoid robotics

## Mission

Our mission is to:
- foster collaboration among robotics practitioners and students
- promote open-source development in robotics
- share technical knowledge and project experience
- support learning and professional growth within the robotics community

## Community

Robotics TEAM KR brings together individuals with diverse backgrounds and experience levels, united by a common interest in robotics and engineering innovation. We encourage collaboration, knowledge sharing, and practical implementation across robotics disciplines.
